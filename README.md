# Features
## Dark/Light Mode
- Dark and Light mode options for your viewing pleasure (or displeasure if you use Light Mode... :P)
- Your preference for Dark or Light mode is automatically saved locally and will remember your preference each time you open your Workspace.

## Scratchpad
- Throw any random little notes you need into the Scratchpad and they are automatically saved LOCALLY, in-case you accidentally close your tab.
- You can copy and clear the notes in your Scratchpad.
- To prevent accidental leaks of PII you can also clear your clipboard.
